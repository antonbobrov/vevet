Class Timeline

Timeline for time-based progress with easing.

  • Operates in the 0 → 1 range with configurable duration and easing
  • play, reverse, pause, and reset control playback
  • Emits update on every progress change plus lifecycle callbacks (start, end, play, pause, reverse, reset, resume)
  • Does not touch the DOM — drive animation from callbacks or progress / eased
